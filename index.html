Caribbean Tents & Pirate Ships

Match every palm island with an adjacent pirate ship!

You Won!

All pirate ships anchored correctly!

How to Play

  • Goal: Assign exactly one pirate ship to every palm island.
  • Placement: Ships must be placed directly adjacent (north, south, east, or west) to their assigned palm island.
  • No Touching: Pirate ships can never touch each other—not even diagonally!
  • Row/Column Numbers: The numbers outside the grid tell you how many ships belong in that row or column. Numbers turn green when full!
  • Controls: Tap a water tile once to place a galleon, tap again for an 'X' (water mark), and tap a third time to clear it.
